Research Interests
======
My research focuses on **Artificial Intelligence for Operations Research (AI4OR)**, with two main directions:

### Large Language Models for Operations Research

- **Optimization problem modeling:** automated formulation of vehicle routing problems and automated reformulation of robust optimization problems.
- **Optimization problem solving:** automated algorithm design and large language models as end-to-end solvers.

I also explore generative AI-assisted theoretical discovery in operations research, including new bounds for Euclidean optimization problems.

### Deep Reinforcement Learning for Combinatorial Optimization

My work on **Neural Combinatorial Optimization** covers:

- **Multi-objective combinatorial optimization:** learning to construct, improve, and predict solutions.
- **Towards foundation models for combinatorial optimization:** training efficiency, cross-task generalization, and cross-distribution generalization.
- **Complex combinatorial optimization:** complex constraints, robust vehicle routing problems, and diverse routing and scheduling variants.
